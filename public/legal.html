Terms of Use, Copyright Compliance & DMCA Notice

FileHunt Studio is a public media metadata inspector, oEmbed stream analyzer, and archival utility designed strictly for authorized, Creative Commons, public domain, and self-owned media resources.

1. Authorized & Non-DRM Media Only

FileHunt does not decrypt, bypass, or circumvent Digital Rights Management (DRM), paywalls, or private access controls. Only publicly accessible metadata and public streams are inspected.

2. User Responsibility & Fair Use

Users are solely responsible for ensuring they hold the necessary rights, creator permission, or applicable Fair Use / Creative Commons license before saving any media file.

3. Zero Permanent Media Hosting

FileHunt operates as a stateless inspection tool. We do not host, index, or permanently store third-party video or audio content on our servers.

DMCA Safe Harbor & Copyright Takedown Procedure

FileHunt respects the intellectual property rights of content creators and complies with the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA). If you are a copyright owner and believe a specific domain or URL should be excluded from our public metadata inspector, please submit a written notice containing:

  • Identification of the copyrighted work or platform domain.
  • The exact URL pattern you wish to block from metadata inspection.
  • Your contact details and a statement of good-faith belief under penalty of perjury.
